Calculation basis and limits

The entered duct dimensions are inside sizes. Development is calculated at the centre of the plate thickness: centre-surface diameter = inside diameter + thickness, and centre-surface rectangular width/depth = corresponding inside size + thickness. The four sides are unfolded around their shared corner generators to produce one continuous plate with a single closing seam.

  • The round opening, including its centre-surface adjustment, must remain within the rectangular opening in plan.
  • Plate thickness is included only for locating the development surface. Seam, flange, kerf, weld-shrinkage and trimming allowances are not yet added.
  • Verify dimensions, allowances, material orientation and fit-up before cutting plate.
