# Industrial Calculation Hub — Master Site Map

## 1. Purpose and publishing model

Industrial Calculation Hub will be a knowledge-first industrial engineering platform. Its public content is organized around four primary domains:

1. **Engineering** — engineering disciplines, principles, and design fundamentals.
2. **Industrial Equipment** — equipment working principles, components, applications, operation, and maintenance fundamentals.
3. **Industrial Processes** — plant/process flows and the application of equipment in power, cement, steel, chemical, and related industries.
4. **Engineering Reference Data** — structured material, dimensional, property, and component databases.

The existing **Engineering Calculators** and **Unit Converters** remain available as utility sections. No new calculator scope is included in this plan.

One subject is published as **one canonical article**. It can appear in several learning tracks or industry sectors through internal links; it must not be copied into separate pages. For example, the Bag Filter article belongs to Air Pollution Control, is linked from the equipment library, and is used by Power, Cement, Steel, and Waste-to-Energy sector pages.

## 2. Reference policy

- Technical explanations are written originally and cite fundamental engineering textbooks.
- CBSE and university syllabi are used only to map topic order and learning level.
- The site is not affiliated with CBSE, any university, or any standards organization.
- Book figures, tables, worked examples, and text are not reproduced.
- Design, loading, structural, safety, and equipment-selection articles carry an educational/preliminary-use disclaimer.
- Code-specific design rules, project specifications, and current legal requirements are outside this textbook-only policy unless a separate standards policy is approved later.

## E11. Database source and maintenance policy

- Every data row must identify its source, edition, unit system, and verification date.
- Data derived from a standard must use the official standard, an authorized public extract, or an openly licensed manufacturer catalogue; it must not be copied from an unauthorized third-party table.
- Where a standard is copyrighted or paid, the site provides the designation, scope, publisher link, and a derived/summary record only where that is legally permitted.
- No unverified "equivalent" material-grade claim is published.
- A material property is always displayed with the product form, condition, test temperature, and source because values vary by condition.
- Standards and dimensional data are reviewed whenever the cited edition changes, at least annually.

Database records may eventually number in the thousands; they are not included in the knowledge-article page-count target.
