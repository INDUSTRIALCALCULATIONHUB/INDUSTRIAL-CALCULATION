Privacy policy

How website information is handled.

This policy explains the information that may be collected through Industrial Calculation Hub, how it may be used and the choices available to visitors.

Last updated: 1 September 2026

⌾

Scope of this policy

This policy applies to information collected through industrialcalculation.com. It should be read together with the Terms and Disclaimer. It does not govern third-party sites that may be linked from this website.

Information that may be collected

Technical and usage information

When visitors use the site, service providers may process technical information such as browser type, device type, IP address, pages viewed, referring page and approximate usage data.

Information you provide

If you contact us by email, we receive the information included in your message, such as your name, email address and the content of the enquiry.

How information may be used

Information may be used to operate and secure the website, understand its performance, respond to enquiries, improve content and functionality, and support advertising or consent functions where they are enabled.

Cookies, advertising and consent

Cookies, device identifiers and similar technologies may be used by the website or third-party providers to support functionality, measure usage and serve advertising. On monetised pages, Google AdSense and its advertising partners may collect, share and use information for ad delivery, measurement and, where a visitor permits it, ads personalisation.

Visitors in the EEA, the United Kingdom and Switzerland are shown a consent-management message before advertising choices are applied where consent is required. The message lets visitors accept, reject or manage relevant choices. A visitor can change browser cookie settings at any time; disabling cookies may affect features and advertising preferences.

For Google's data practices, read How Google uses information from sites and apps that use its services. Third-party providers process information under their own policies.

Third-party services and links

Third-party providers process information under their own privacy policies and terms. Links to external websites are provided for convenience; Industrial Calculation Hub is not responsible for their content or privacy practices. Review those third-party policies before supplying information to them.

Retention, security and your choices

We retain contact information only as long as reasonably needed to respond to the enquiry, maintain records or meet applicable obligations. Reasonable safeguards are used, but no internet transmission or storage system is completely secure.

Where applicable law gives you privacy rights, you may contact us to request information, correction or deletion relating to information you have provided directly. We may need to verify the request before responding.

Children's privacy

This website is intended for engineers, students, professionals and general adult users. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy when website practices, services or applicable requirements change. The current version and its update date will be posted on this page.

For privacy questions, email contact@industrialcalculation.com or use the Contact page.
