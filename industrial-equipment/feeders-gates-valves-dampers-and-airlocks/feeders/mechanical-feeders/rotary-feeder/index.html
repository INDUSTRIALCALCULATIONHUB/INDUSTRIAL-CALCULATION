What Is Rotary Feeder?

Rotary Feeder is an industrial equipment topic within Feeders. It explains the equipment’s function, main interfaces and operating considerations when the required duty is to control the withdrawal, isolation or regulated discharge of bulk material.

In this hierarchy, it is treated as bulk-material feeding, isolation or flow-control equipment. A practical review starts with the required duty and then checks the equipment’s flow path, interfaces and constraints.

Why Is It Important in Industrial Equipment?

Rotary Feeder should be considered as part of a complete feeders arrangement, not as an isolated item. Its actual configuration depends on the process duty, material or fluid basis, interfaces, operating conditions and applicable project requirements.
