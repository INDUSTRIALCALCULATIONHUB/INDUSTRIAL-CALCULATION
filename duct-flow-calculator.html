Engineering Knowledge

What is Duct Flow Rate?

Duct flow rate is the volume of air passing through a duct per unit time and is one of the most important parameters in HVAC systems, industrial ventilation, dust collection systems, clean rooms, power plants, and Air Pollution Control (APC) equipment. It is commonly expressed in m³/s, m³/hr, or CFM and is used for duct sizing, fan selection, pressure loss calculations, and system balancing.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is duct flow rate?

Duct flow rate is the volume of air flowing through a duct per unit time. It is commonly expressed in m³/s, m³/hr, or CFM and is a key parameter in HVAC and industrial ventilation system design.

Can this calculator be used for HVAC duct sizing?

Yes. It is suitable for preliminary HVAC duct sizing, industrial ventilation systems, dust collection systems, and process air applications.

Does this calculator calculate pressure loss?

No. This calculator determines airflow, duct size, and air velocity only. Pressure loss calculations should be performed separately during detailed duct system design.

Can the calculated airflow be used for fan selection?

Yes. The calculated airflow can be used as an input for preliminary fan selection. Final fan selection should also consider total static pressure, system resistance, and operating conditions.
