Who We Are

Industrial Calculation Hub is a free online engineering platform developed to provide practical engineering calculators, unit converters, and technical resources for engineers, consultants, students, and industrial professionals. Our goal is to simplify complex engineering calculations by offering reliable, easy-to-use tools that save time and improve productivity.

The website focuses on real industrial applications across mechanical engineering, process engineering, HVAC systems, power plants, fabrication, piping, material handling, boilers, and EPC projects. We continuously expand our collection of engineering tools and technical articles to support engineers throughout design, estimation, execution, and maintenance activities.

Engineering Experience and Technical Background

Industrial Calculation Hub is created to provide practical engineering resources based on real industrial applications and engineering practices. The platform focuses on mechanical, process, thermal, piping, equipment design, Air Pollution Control (APC), and EPC engineering topics.

The content covers engineering calculations, equipment fundamentals, system design concepts, and industrial applications commonly used in power plants, cement plants, steel industries, chemical industries, and manufacturing facilities.

The purpose of this platform is to help engineers, designers, consultants, students, and technical professionals understand engineering principles and apply them effectively in practical situations.

Content Development Approach

Engineering calculators and technical articles published on Industrial Calculation Hub are developed using established engineering equations, standard calculation methods, and practical design considerations.

Each resource is prepared with focus on calculation methodology, engineering assumptions, application areas, limitations, and practical understanding rather than only providing numerical results.

The website is continuously improved by adding new engineering tools, expanding technical guides, reviewing existing calculations, and improving usability for engineering professionals.
