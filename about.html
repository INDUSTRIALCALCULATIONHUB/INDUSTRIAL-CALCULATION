About the platform

Engineering knowledge for practical industry.

Industrial Calculation Hub brings together original engineering knowledge, existing calculation tools and reference data for learning, preliminary evaluation and responsible use.

Last reviewed: 23 August 2026

⌘

What we are building

The approved site structure is organised around Engineering, Industrial Equipment, Industrial Processes and Engineering Reference Data. It is intended to help students and professionals progress from engineering fundamentals to equipment, process and data applications.

Our initial construction priority is knowledge pages. Existing calculators and unit converters remain available while new knowledge pages are developed under the frozen hierarchy.

How content is developed

Original editorial work

Content is written for Industrial Calculation Hub. Fundamental books guide the subject matter, but their protected text, diagrams and tables are not reproduced without permission.

Practical engineering context

Topics are connected to equipment, systems, fabrication, operations and preliminary engineering work so the learning has industrial relevance.

Clear limitations

Examples, calculations and data must state their assumptions and limits. They do not replace project-specific engineering verification.

Connected learning

Related links, definitions, references and further-reading paths are used to connect school-level science through advanced engineering subjects.

Who the platform supports

Students

Readers building foundations in science and engineering disciplines.

Engineering professionals

Design, process, mechanical, thermal, project and plant engineers.

Industrial teams

Consultants, fabricators, EPC teams and technical professionals across industrial sectors.
