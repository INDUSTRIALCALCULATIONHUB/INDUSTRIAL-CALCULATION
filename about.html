Our purpose

Make engineering information easier to use

We organise industrial engineering knowledge in a clear structure so readers can move from principles to practical application.

What the platform covers

The approved site structure is organised around Engineering, Industrial Equipment, Industrial Processes and Engineering Reference Data.

Coverage includes fundamentals, mechanics, thermal and process topics, equipment, material handling, power and industrial applications, as well as standards and dimensional reference data.

Who it is for

A reference point for learning and practice

The material is prepared for engineering students and working professionals who need a practical, well-organised starting point.

Readers we support

  • Students building foundations from science through engineering disciplines.
  • Design, project, process, mechanical, thermal and plant engineers.
  • Consultants, fabricators, EPC teams and technical professionals.
  • Readers exploring power, cement, steel, chemical and manufacturing applications.

Important use note

Information on this website is educational and for reference or preliminary evaluation. Verify inputs, applicable standards, calculations and final designs independently before any safety-critical, commercial or project use.
