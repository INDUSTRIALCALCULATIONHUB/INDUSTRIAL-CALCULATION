Engineering knowledge

Pump power, efficiency and motor selection

Pump selection starts with the required flow and total dynamic head. The calculated hydraulic power must be increased to allow for pump and motor losses, then a practical motor rating is selected with a suitable design margin.

Total dynamic head must already include static head and all relevant system losses.

Formula and inputs

Hydraulic power

Ph = ρgQH

For water, with Q in m³/s, the existing calculator reports the result in kW.

Motor input

Pm = Ph / (ηp × ηm)

ηp and ηm are the entered pump and motor efficiencies.

Design power

Pd = Pm × (1 + margin)

The next available motor rating is selected from the existing list.

Assumptions and limitations

  • The implemented calculation uses water density and a constant gravitational acceleration.
  • It does not determine actual pump curve performance, NPSH, motor starting method, speed, service factor or electrical supply.
  • Final motor and pump selection must use manufacturer curves and project requirements.

Frequently asked questions

Does the calculator calculate pipe friction?

No. Include all pipeline losses in the entered total dynamic head.

Is the recommended motor a final selection?

No. It is a preliminary standard-rating recommendation only.
