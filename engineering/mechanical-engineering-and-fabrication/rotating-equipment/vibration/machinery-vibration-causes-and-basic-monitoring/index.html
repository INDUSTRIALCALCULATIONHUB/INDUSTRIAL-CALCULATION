What Is Machinery Vibration: Causes and Basic Monitoring?

Machinery vibration is motion around an equilibrium position caused by rotating unbalance, misalignment, looseness, resonance, hydraulic or aerodynamic forces and other mechanisms. Trend monitoring helps identify abnormal change.

Why Is It Important in Engineering?

A vibration reading is an indicator, not a diagnosis by itself. Interpretation requires machine speed, measurement position, operating condition, history and knowledge of the machine’s construction and support system.
